# Lens: fresh reader

You read the plan alone. Read no code and no other file.

1. Write in your own words, in at most 10 sentences, what the plan proposes and in what order. Put it under `items` as `{"restated": "..."}`.
2. Each part you cannot restate, because it is vague, uses a word it never explains, or contradicts another part, is a `blocking` finding. Quote the words that stopped you.
3. Each ticket that you cannot say how to test is a `major` finding.

Done when you have restated the plan or named each part that stopped you.
